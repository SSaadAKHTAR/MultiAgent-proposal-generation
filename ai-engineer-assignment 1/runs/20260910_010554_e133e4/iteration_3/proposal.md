# Executive Summary

Northwind Logistics has achieved an impressive 40% growth over the past year, expanding its operations to support approximately 600 contracted drivers and 40 dispatchers. However, the operational infrastructure has not scaled alongside this growth. Manual data entry, disconnected systems, and technical debt are creating significant workflow bottlenecks, frustrating drivers, and causing invoicing delays. 

This proposal outlines a 6-month Phase 1 engagement designed to streamline dispatcher workflows, resolve data discrepancies, and modernize the driver experience. By executing this initiative, Northwind Logistics can expect to reduce dispatch time per load by 30%—saving the equivalent of four additional dispatcher headcounts—and return to a five-day Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), which will free up approximately $1.4M in working capital. Leveraging our recent experience resolving similar challenges for peer freight brokerages like Cascade Freight, we are uniquely positioned to partner with your team to deliver these operational and financial wins.

# Understanding

Based on our initial discussions, we understand that Northwind Logistics is operating a complex technology stack, including a custom Rails 5 dispatch application, Salesforce (Sales Cloud) for CRM, NetSuite for accounting, and a custom React Native driver mobile app. Recent acquisitions have left technology integrations incomplete, exacerbating operational strain.

The primary challenges driving this engagement include:
*   **Operational Inefficiency:** Dispatchers are overwhelmed by manual data entry between the custom dispatch tool and Salesforce. Two previous internal attempts to resolve these workflow issues have failed.
*   **Financial Delays:** Finance is currently three weeks behind on invoicing due to persistent data discrepancies across systems.
*   **Driver Dissatisfaction:** Contracted drivers are highly dissatisfied with the current state of the mobile application.
*   **Compliance Pressures:** An upcoming federal Electronic Logging Device (ELD) mandate update in November poses a significant risk. Missing this deadline could result in severe fines and the potential loss of operating authority in certain states. 

# Approach

Our approach to this Phase 1 engagement is designed to deliver immediate operational relief while building a foundation for future scalability. We will focus on the following key pillars:

1.  **Workflow Automation & Data Integrity:** We will map and streamline the data flow between the dispatch workflows, Salesforce, and NetSuite. The primary focus will be eliminating manual dual-entry and ensuring that Finance receives accurate, timely data to accelerate invoicing and reduce DSO.
2.  **Architecture & Future-Proofing:** Recognizing the technical debt in the current environment, we will design a solution where 60-70% of the newly developed workflow logic is portable and can survive any future platform migrations. 
3.  **Technical Alignment:** All proposed architectural changes will be subject to comprehensive review and formal sign-off by the CTO to ensure alignment with Northwind's broader technical strategy.
4.  **Coordinated Mobile Enhancements:** Any necessary updates to the driver mobile app will be strictly coordinated with the ongoing ELD compliance workstream to ensure our workflow improvements do not interfere with regulatory requirements.

# Phases & Timeline

This 6-month Phase 1 engagement will be structured to deliver rapid value while mitigating technical risk. 

*   **Phase 1A: Discovery & Architectural Design**
    *   Map existing data flows between the dispatch tool, Salesforce, and NetSuite.
    *   Draft architecture proposals for CTO review and approval.
    *   Coordinate with the Head of Compliance to map dependencies with the ELD mandate.
*   **Phase 1B: Workflow Automation & Integration**
    *   Implement data synchronization between CRM and dispatch operations.
    *   Develop portable workflow logic to reduce dispatcher manual entry by 30%.
*   **Phase 1C: Financial Alignment & Driver App Updates**
    *   Resolve data discrepancies blocking NetSuite invoicing.
    *   Address critical usability issues in the React Native driver app.
*   **Phase 1D: Pilot & Production Rollout**
    *   Deploy the integrated solution to a pilot group of dispatchers and drivers.
    *   Monitor data accuracy for Finance to validate the return to a five-day DSO.

# Open Questions

*   **Budget Cap:** There is currently a discrepancy regarding the approved budget. The CFO has noted a firm $300,000 cap for Phase 1, while the VP of Operations indicated an expected range of $250,000 to $400,000.
*   **Core Technical Approach:** We need to align on the foundational strategy for the dispatch tool. The CTO has expressed a desire to replace the legacy system with a SaaS platform, whereas the VP of Operations prefers incrementally fixing workflows on the existing system.
*   **Pilot Timeline:** The CFO and VP of Operations require a pilot in production by the end of Q3 (September 30) for budget allocation reasons, but the CTO believes Q4 is a more realistic target.
*   **ELD Compliance Details:** We currently lack specific details regarding the upcoming federal ELD mandate update. We need to review these requirements thoroughly to ensure our mobile and workflow updates do not conflict with compliance initiatives.

**Recommendation:** To resolve these differing internal requirements regarding the budget cap, core technical approach, and pilot timeline, we strongly recommend scheduling a joint alignment meeting with the CFO, CTO, and VP of Operations. Achieving consensus on these points will allow us to confidently lock in the timeline, technical scope, and final fixed-fee structure.
